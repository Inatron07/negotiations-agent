Negotiations Agent (PoC) — Read Me

An AI buyer copilot for the procurement negotiation workflow, grounded on real purchase requisition (PR), vendor bid, and market-intelligence data via Automation Anywhere's EKB (Enterprise Knowledge Base) GenAI agent layer.

Features

  • PR & vendor bid review — line-by-line commercial evaluation, normalized for freight, GST, warranty, delivery and payment terms.
  • Market benchmarking — compares evaluated vendor rates against market-median pricing and suggests negotiation targets.
  • Outlier & vendor risk detection — flags price outliers and assesses vendor financial/delivery risk and dependency.
  • Negotiation strategy & playbooks — recommends an approach (monitor, controlled negotiation, or auction) with target and stretch pricing.
  • Auction recommendations — checks auction eligibility and suggests a format (reverse, Japanese, or Dutch).

How to use

  • Type a question about a PR, vendor, market benchmark, or negotiation strategy — or tap a quick-action chip to jump straight in.
  • Use New chat in the sidebar to start a fresh conversation; past chats stay in the history list and can be reopened any time.
  • Answers are AI-generated and grounded on the loaded procurement data — always verify before acting on a recommendation.
